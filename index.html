About justGoron

Programmer by day, Zoo builder and wildlife enthusiast by night! I love exploring zoo design through a creative and educational lens. My channel combines real-world zoos and wildlife encounters, with creative and realistic builds in the game Planet Zoo.

While everything started with Planet Zoo, and it still lies at its core, this game has awakened a much deeper interest in my in not only zoos, but wildlife conservation in general. I love to share this passion with the world, whether it be through showcasing real-world zoos as inspiration for the game itself, building large-scale projects, or sharing quick design tricks, the goal is always to create content that helps other players build better, more intentional zoos, and get the most out of the game.

Thank you for visiting my site and following my journey in both the virtual and real worlds of zoos and wildlife.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Planet Zoo?

Planet Zoo is a simulation game where you build and manage your own zoo. It combines business management with creative building, allowing players to design realistic habitats. I almost exclusively focus on creating content for this game, in the "sandbox" mode where you have unlimited money and resoruces to create the zoo of your dreams.

I have a custom built computer that I've made myself (okay I might have had some help from friends). It features a AMD Ryzen 7 9700X processor, 64GB of RAM, and an NVIDIA RTX 5070Ti graphics card.

What is the random inspiration tool?

The random inspiration tool helps pick a random real-world animal habitat from my Real Life Inspiration series. It's designed to give you an idea of what to make when you're not sure what to build next.

How often do you post new videos?

Upload frequency varies by project and series. I try to post weekly on fridays, and stream on wednesdays, but sometimes life gets in the way. Make sure to subscribe to stay posted, or join my discord server to get notified of new content!
